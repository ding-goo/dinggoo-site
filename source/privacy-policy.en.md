Privacy Policy — DING GOO

Last updated: 23 October 2026
Service provider: DING GOO, United Arab Emirates
Contact: support@dinggoo.app

DING GOO is an app for parents that turns school messages into organised tasks for each child. We collect only the minimum needed to run it.


What we collect

- Your email address: to sign in with a code sent to it, so there are no passwords. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only your name, your email and an identifier for the account, and from Apple a hidden email it creates if you choose that.
- Your display name (such as "Aisha's mum") and your relationship (mother / father / guardian), if you enter them in your profile.
- Your children's details that you enter: first name, grade and class, and "boy / girl" if you choose (only to word the texts). We do not ask for a child's date of birth, phone, email or location.
- School messages you choose to share with the app. If you share a photo or a PDF, the text is read on your device and only the text reaches us. Unless you choose "Attach the photos as a worksheet" when sharing (the option is off every time), in which case the photos themselves reach us so they appear in the task.
- Worksheets: photos and PDF files you attach to a task (by taking a photo, from Photos, from Files, or when sharing). Before saving, we remove location and camera data from photos, on your device and then on our server too. They are kept in a private file store that is not public, and open only through a temporary link valid for 5 minutes, for you and your child's device. We do not analyse worksheets and do not send them to Anthropic.
- Explanation videos: links to YouTube videos you add to a task. We keep the video number, its title if you wrote one, and how much of each video your child watched (as a percentage).
- Stars, streak, challenges and badges: the tasks your child completed, the stars they earned, their streak days, their progress in challenges, their badges, and the freeze day you set.
- Achievement cards: the numbers on the card your child sends to your app (streak, stars, and the number of tasks and videos in the month), with no pictures or text.
- Tasks and subjects: the tasks extracted or that you add, their dates, and the subjects you add or hide.
- The notification token for your device (encrypted), to send you reminders.
- Your subscription status as confirmed by Apple. We never see your card details.


What stays on your device only and never reaches us

- Your child's photo: encrypted on your device, without location data, and not copied to iCloud.
- Textbooks (PDF files): they stay on the device you added them to. Sending them to your child's device happens directly between the two devices over AirDrop, without passing through our servers. They may be included in your device's iCloud backup, depending on your settings.
- When a worksheet is shown on your device or your child's: it is shown from memory only, not saved on the device, and cleared when you sign out.


How we use the data

Only to run the service: extracting tasks, showing them to you and your child, and reminding you of them. We do not sell data, do not show ads, do not track you across apps or websites, and do not use any third-party analytics or advertising tools.


Artificial intelligence (Anthropic)

We send the text of a school message you shared to Anthropic, the company that develops the Claude model, to extract tasks and dates. Before sending, we replace your children's names with codes (S1, S2…) and remove phone numbers and email addresses. We send with it only the child's grade and the names of their subjects. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and deletes it automatically within 30 days. It keeps it longer only if its systems detect a breach of its usage policy, or if the law requires it.


YouTube videos

When your child or you play an explanation video you added, the video loads from YouTube (Google) inside the app, in YouTube's "privacy-enhanced mode" (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube then receives what any website receives: the device's IP address and type, and the video being watched, and Google's privacy policy applies to that. We do not send YouTube your child's name or any data from your account. Nothing loads from YouTube until a video is tapped, and nothing YouTube stores stays on the device after the player closes. On a child's device only the video you chose opens, it closes when it ends, and it cannot lead on to YouTube. When paused, YouTube may show suggestions for other videos inside the player; we cannot prevent that, and they cannot be opened from the app.


Others who process data on our behalf

- Railway: hosting the server, the database and the worksheet store, in Europe.
- Resend: sending the sign-in code to your email.
- Apple: Sign in with Apple, subscriptions and payment, and delivering notifications (the notification text passes through Apple).
- Google: Sign in with Google, when you choose it (name, email and account identifier only).
- Google (YouTube): playing explanation videos only, when a video is tapped.

Some of this data may be processed outside the United Arab Emirates (including Europe and the United States), by providers committed to protecting it.


How long we keep data

- The text of a school message: 30 days after it is analysed, then deleted. A fingerprint from which the text cannot be recovered remains, to prevent duplicate tasks.
- Sign-in codes and device-linking codes: 24 hours.
- In-app notifications: 90 days.
- Worksheets: 30 days after the task's due date (or after upload if the task has no date), then deleted automatically from the store. They are deleted immediately if you delete the task or the worksheet. Photos attached when sharing that did not become a task: two days.
- Explanation videos: with the task; deleted when you delete them or the task.
- Stars, streak, challenges, badges and achievement cards: as long as the account exists; deleted when the account is deleted.
- Tasks, children's profiles and your account: until you delete them or delete your account.
- The security log (sign-ins, deletions and subscriptions, without the content of your messages): one year, then deleted automatically.


Children

DING GOO is aimed at parents. A child does not create an account or enter an email. The parent links the child's device with a QR code, and the child then sees only their own tasks, books, worksheets and the explanation videos the parent chose, with no access to the subscription, the settings or their siblings' data. You can unlink any device at any time.


Signing in with Apple or Google

When you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only your name, your email address and an identifier for the account, to create your account and sign you in. We do not receive your password, your contacts or any other data from your account. You can change your "display name" and "relationship" in your profile at any time.


Stars, streak, challenges and badges

To encourage your child, we keep: the tasks they completed, the stars they earned, their streak days, their progress in challenges, their badges, and the freeze day set by the parent. This data is shown only to the child and their parent; it is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone.


Explanation videos

The parent chooses the explanation videos from YouTube. We keep how much of each video the child watched, to award stars and show progress. The videos play in YouTube's player inside the app, and playback is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.


Learning portal link

A parent can save the link to the child's school portal (LMS). The link opens inside the app in Safari, and what the child types there stays between the child and the portal; we do not see their account there and do not store their sign-in details.


Achievement cards

The achievement summary card is sent from the child's device to their parent's app, and the child's device has no button to share it with others. If the child chooses "Save image", it is added to the device's Photos app (with add-only permission, without seeing your photos), and from then on it follows the device's settings. The parent can share the card from their own app if they wish.


Data kept on your device

The app keeps a copy of your latest tasks and task lists on your device so they show without internet, and the Home Screen widgets show today's tasks, the streak and the next exam from another, small copy. Both copies are encrypted, are not included in backups, contain no sign-in data, and are deleted when you sign out.


Downloading your data

From "Privacy & Data" you can download a copy of your data in a single file (JSON) that includes your tasks, your children, your subjects and the list of worksheets, without the files themselves and without any codes or secret keys.


Deleting your account and your rights

- Delete your account inside the app: Settings ⚙️ → "Delete account". All sessions are cancelled immediately.
- Within one hour your family's data is erased completely: children, tasks, messages, devices and worksheets (from the database and from the file store). If the family has another parent, the family stays with them and only your own data is deleted.
- Backups: the database keeps emergency backups, and we sometimes keep a copy on an encrypted device held by the developer before a server update. All these copies are deleted automatically within 35 days at most, so your data may remain in them until then and then disappear.
- You may ask for a copy of your data or for it to be corrected at support@dinggoo.app.


Security

Encryption in transit, and each family's data kept apart inside the database. Sign-in codes and sessions are stored on our side as fingerprints, not as text, and the sign-in details on your device are kept in the Keychain.


Changes

We will let you know inside the app about any material change before it takes effect.
